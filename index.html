Quick Start Guide

1

Make a Copy of the Google Sheet

Choose your desired tool on the left and click the Copy Template Google Sheet button. This opens the template in Google Sheets so you can make your own editable copy.

2

Locate & Copy your Sheet ID

Look at your browser's address bar when viewing your copied Google Sheet. Copy the entire ID string between /d/ and /edit:

https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/ 1NEymr4St_ZgL5UUW_h4TGO8xbcy20Bu7V9edCuuIWkU /edit#gid=0
The highlighted text in yellow above is the complete Sheet ID
3

Paste Sheet ID or URL & Inputs

Paste the copied Sheet ID (or full URL) into the input box on the left, along with your target Website URL or comma-separated Keywords.

4

Submit & Track Results

Hit Submit Request. The workflow triggers in the background and populates your Google Sheet with structured findings in under a minute.
