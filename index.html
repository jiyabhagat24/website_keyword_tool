Quick Start Guide

1

Make a Copy of the Google Sheet

Pick a tool with the toggle on the left and click Copy Template Google Sheet. This opens the template so you can make your own editable copy.

2

Locate & Copy your Sheet ID

Look at the browser address bar on your copied sheet. Copy the ID string between /d/ and /edit:

https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/ 1NEymr4St_ZgL5UUW_h4TGO8xbcy20Bu7V9edCuuIWkU /edit#gid=0
The highlighted text above is the complete Sheet ID.
3

Paste Sheet ID or URL & Inputs

Paste the Sheet ID (or full URL) into the input box on the left, along with your target Website URL or comma-separated Keywords.

4

Submit & Track Results

Hit Submit Request. The workflow runs in the background and populates your Google Sheet in under a minute.
